# Gate 39: Restricted Owner Live Classroom and Courseware

## Owner decision

The Florida Class D LMS is instructor led and separate from the self-paced Obserra Academy Learners World. The FDACS course uses live video instruction and instructor-controlled courseware. Academy courses may later be purchased for self-paced completion only after each Academy release is loaded and approved.

## Implemented owner-test scope

The exact-release, AAL2 owner-review surface now provides:

- one private Daily instructor room with camera, microphone, screen sharing, people controls, chat, reactions, and hand raising;
- three owner-controlled learner views that start with camera and microphone off, may enable their own media, and cannot administer or screen share;
- instructor authority to mute or remove learner-view participants through Daily room administration;
- private upload and protected retrieval for PPTX, PDF, PNG, JPG, WEBP, MP4, and WEBM courseware up to 100 MB;
- in-LMS presentation of PDFs, images, and video, with previous/next media controls;
- protected PowerPoint retrieval for full-screen PowerPoint presentation through the instructor screen-share control; and
- explicit room and courseware cleanup controls.

The owner-review room and courseware actions create no learner, enrollment, attendance, instructional-time, exam, completion, certificate, or LIAS record. They award no training credit and do not authorize regulated production.
