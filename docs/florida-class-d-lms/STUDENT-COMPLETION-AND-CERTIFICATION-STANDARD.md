# Florida Class D Student Completion and Certification Standard

## Purpose

This controlled standard governs how completion, examination status, certificates, and Florida application documents are described in all student-facing materials for the Obserra Executive Protection & Intelligence LLC Florida Class D Security Officer Training program.

It applies to LMS onboarding, student handbooks, course syllabi, lesson materials, attendance notices, examination instructions, completion screens, FAQs, application guidance, instructor materials, regulatory submission documentation, and any future marketing or enrollment materials.

## Mandatory student-facing completion rule

**Completing 40 instructional hours does not, by itself, constitute successful course completion and does not cause any course-completion certificate to be issued.**

A learner reaches successful completion only after all controlled requirements are satisfied:

1. The learner's identity has been verified through the regulated enrollment process.
2. The LMS and instructor records show at least 2,400 verified instructional minutes across five qualifying training days, with 480 verified instructional minutes credited for each required day after any approved make-up reconciliation.
3. All 18 required curriculum areas and required learning checks are complete, and required remediation is closed.
4. The learner completes the separately controlled 170-question final examination and earns a passing score of at least 128 correct answers.
5. There are no unresolved attendance, presence, examination, security, or remediation issues that block completion.
6. An authorized school/compliance review confirms the evidence and approves successful completion.

Until all six conditions are satisfied, the learner remains incomplete and **no Obserra course-completion certificate and no official Florida training certificate may be released to the learner**.

## Certificate sequence after a passing examination

After the learner has passed the final examination and the school has approved successful completion:

- The LMS may automatically create the learner-specific **supplemental Obserra Course Completion Certificate** and application-handoff record from the controlled completion record.
- The supplemental certificate must use the learner's verified legal name and controlled completion data. It is a school record and does not replace the official Florida training certificate.
- Authorized school staff report successful completion through the official FDACS LIAS process within the applicable reporting window.
- The official **FDACS-16103 Certificate of Security Officer Training** is generated through LIAS. Obserra does not synthesize, imitate, or self-generate the official state form.
- After the LIAS certificate is confirmed and securely ingested, the learner may download the official FDACS-16103 from the protected completion-document portal.

## Student application package

The student completion-document portal must clearly identify which materials are intended for the learner's Class D application record. The portal may provide:

- the official LIAS-generated FDACS-16103 Certificate of Security Officer Training;
- the supplemental Obserra Course Completion Certificate;
- controlled Class D application instructions and links to the official FDACS application process.

The student must be told that the official FDACS-16103 is the state training-completion record and that the supplemental Obserra certificate does not substitute for it.

## Student information permitted on completion records

Learner-specific completion records may use protected information already present in the regulated record system when required for accurate issuance. At minimum, the supplemental Obserra certificate should display the learner's verified legal name, course title, 40 instructional hours, successful completion date, and a unique certificate/reference identifier. Other protected identifiers should be minimized and should not be exposed on public pages or in public source code.

Date of birth, identity-document details, raw verification evidence, Clerk identifiers, internal enrollment UUIDs, examination answer data, and other sensitive regulated records must not be printed on a public-facing certificate unless a specific regulatory requirement later requires them.

## Examination failure and retest status

A learner who completes the 40 instructional hours but does not pass the final examination has **not successfully completed the course**. No completion certificate is issued. The learner enters the controlled remediation/retest workflow, and every examination attempt remains preserved in the regulated record. A certificate can be issued only after a later qualifying passing examination and successful completion review.

## Required wording across course materials

Student-facing materials should use substantially the following language:

> Forty instructional hours are required, but hours alone do not complete the course. You must also complete all required learning activities, pass the separate 170-question final examination with at least 128 correct answers, clear any required remediation or attendance issues, and receive school completion approval. No course-completion certificate or FDACS training certificate is released before successful examination and completion approval.

Materials must also state:

> Successful course completion is not the same as issuance of a Florida Class D Security Officer license. The learner must complete the applicable FDACS licensing process and receive the license from the State of Florida before performing duties that require the license.

## Documentation and screenshot evidence for the Class DS submission package

The Class DS LMS submission guide must document this completion standard and include controlled development or production screenshots, as applicable, showing:

1. the learner's 40-hour/five-day completion status without premature certificate issuance;
2. final-examination eligibility and the 128/170 passing requirement;
3. the compliance completion-review screen showing the evidence required before approval;
4. the manual LIAS reporting/certificate-confirmation workflow;
5. the protected student Completion Documents portal distinguishing the official FDACS-16103 from the supplemental Obserra certificate.

Development screenshots must be labeled as development/submission previews and must not be presented as regulatory approval or production evidence. Final production screenshots should replace development previews before the guide is used as operational evidence after applicable authorization and production validation.

## Change-control rule

No course page, handbook, syllabus, lesson deck, instructor guide, FAQ, certificate workflow, or application instruction may state or imply that merely completing 40 hours automatically earns a certificate. Any future change to the completion or certification rule must be reconciled against current Florida requirements and the controlled LMS completion logic before publication.
