# Florida Class D Gate 10 Handoff — Make-Up Training and Time Reconciliation

## Scope

This controlled handoff governs the next regulated Florida Class D LMS increment for Obserra Executive Protection & Intelligence LLC. It remains separate from the commercial Academy course-production workstream.

Gate 10 covers assignment, student/instructor communication, evidence review, and controlled reconciliation of instructional time missed during the live Class D course. Public launch remains disabled while the Class DS application is pending.

## Regulatory design boundary

The workflow is designed to preserve live attendance evidence and to keep make-up credit separate, reviewable, and auditable. Recorded make-up delivery is capped at 600 minutes per student. Recorded instruction will not be activated until the protected playback, timing, participation, and instructor-question controls are completed in a later gate.

## Gate 10 target controls

- staff-only make-up assignment;
- assignment tied to enrollment, instructional day, module, and optional source live session;
- separate live-make-up and recorded-make-up methods;
- 600-minute recorded-make-up cap;
- student question channel to a Class DI instructor;
- instructor answers retained with the regulated record;
- evidence reference and evidence timestamps required before instructional credit;
- no make-up credit beyond the remaining daily or 40-hour instructional deficit;
- separate make-up attendance evidence without rewriting the original live-attendance record;
- correlation identifiers and append-only audit events;
- fail-closed production activation behind Class DS and protected runtime configuration.

## Production boundary

Source implementation and CI do not constitute FDACS approval or production authorization. No public payment, regulated access, final examination, completion issuance, or LIAS execution is enabled by this gate.
