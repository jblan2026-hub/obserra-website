## HeyGen module summaries

### Module 1: Decision context

Target duration: 120 to 150 seconds.

Brief:

```text
Explain why cybersecurity begins with mission, business, people, data, and operational context. Introduce assets, threats, vulnerabilities, controls, consequences, and decision ownership. Close by asking learners to identify one business process whose security failure would materially affect trust, safety, revenue, or operations.
```

### Module 2: Evidence and risk

Target duration: 120 to 150 seconds.

Brief:

```text
Explain the difference among observations, indicators, assumptions, likelihood, impact, and uncertainty. Show why a security professional should not overstate evidence. Close with a prompt asking learners what additional evidence they would seek before recommending action.
```

### Module 3: Control and authority

Target duration: 120 to 150 seconds.

Brief:

```text
Explain preventive, detective, corrective, and recovery controls; who owns risk decisions; why least privilege and identity protection matter; and when escalation is required. Close with a prompt asking learners to identify the accountable owner for a common access decision.
```

### Module 4: Scenario practice

Target duration: 120 to 150 seconds.

Brief:

```text
Present an explicitly instructional scenario involving a suspicious message, unexpected access request, or abnormal account behavior. Walk through evidence preservation, safe reporting, containment boundaries, and escalation without representing the scenario as a historical event.
```

### Module 5: Action and improvement

Target duration: 120 to 150 seconds.

Brief:

```text
Summarize incident reporting, lessons learned, measurable improvement, personal responsibility, and continuous learning. Direct learners to complete the workbook, verify every lesson, and prepare for the final assessment.
```
