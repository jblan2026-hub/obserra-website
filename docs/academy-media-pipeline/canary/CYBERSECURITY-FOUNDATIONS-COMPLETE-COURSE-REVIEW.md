## Course description

Cybersecurity Foundations for New Professionals is a practical introductory course for learners beginning cybersecurity work or assuming greater responsibility for information, systems, identities, operations, and organizational trust. The course connects cybersecurity decisions to business and mission consequences. Learners practice recognizing risk indicators, protecting identities and information, evaluating evidence, reporting suspicious activity, preserving evidence, understanding decision authority, and building repeatable habits that strengthen resilience.

The course uses substantive instruction, realistic fictional or composite scenarios, guided exercises, downloadable job aids, authoritative primary sources, module knowledge checks, reflection activities, and a final assessment. It is professional education and a record of course completion. It does not confer professional certification, licensure, regulatory approval, or authority to act outside an assigned role.
