### Anti robotic controls

1. No uninterrupted avatar segment longer than 20 seconds.
2. Presenter screen time between 35 and 55 percent.
3. Cinematic visual time between 45 and 65 percent.
4. Natural breathing, pauses, emphasis, blinking, eye contact, and gestures.
5. Likeness, voice similarity, pronunciation, and lip synchronization approval.
6. Scene plan, shot list, storyboard, and edit decision list.
7. 1920 by 1080 minimum master, Rec.709 color, and 48 kHz audio.
8. Captions, transcript, rights evidence, and owner approval.
9. LearnWorlds desktop and mobile playback approval.
10. No quality reduction because credits are low.

### Pollo cinematic controls

1. Five module specific visual packs per course.
2. At least four distinct visual contexts per module.
3. Motivated camera movement and lighting.
4. Physically plausible people, objects, and motion.
5. Consistent people, wardrobe, locations, and props.
6. No generated text inside scenes.
7. No third party logos, public figures, or unauthorized likenesses.
8. No generic looping stock look.
9. No morphing anatomy, faces, hands, text, or objects.
10. No excessive neon or science fiction gimmicks.
